J.A.R.V.I.S. — install guide
1. Upload this whole folder to Cloudflare Pages (free): dash.cloudflare.com > Workers & Pages > Create > Pages > Upload assets.
2. Open the https address you get in Chrome or Edge (Android Chrome too). Click the Install button in the top bar.
3. iPhone: open in Safari > Share > Add to Home Screen.
4. First run: tap INITIALIZE, allow Microphone and Notifications when asked.
Notes: browsers cannot launch every native program. Jarvis opens websites and app links (WhatsApp, YouTube, Maps, mail, phone, SMS...) and Windows system apps (Calculator, Settings, Camera).
Reminders alert while Jarvis is open or installed and running; fully closed apps cannot ring without a push server.
AI brain: the free Cloudflare Worker already used by OntechBot. Change it in Settings > AI endpoint.
